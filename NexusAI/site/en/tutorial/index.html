For teachers

Five steps get the course ready. The most important one is uploading the material, because NexusAI only answers with what the teacher uploads.

1. Turn NexusAI on in the course

In the course menu (sometimes under “More”), open NexusAI in this course, tick Use NexusAI in this course and save.

While it is off, students see nothing of NexusAI in the course. Turning it off deletes nothing: the history, questions and documents come back when you turn it on again.

2. Upload the material

In the course menu, go to NexusAI · Materials. Drag your files, one or several at a time (PDF, DOCX, PPTX, XLSX, CSV, MD, HTML or TXT, up to 20 MB each), and choose:

  • Course unit: the course section the file goes into.
  • Visible to students: whether students can see it right away.

Each file is added to the course as a File activity in that unit and goes from “processing” to “indexed”. From then on, the chat, search and quizzes use it.

NexusAI only answers with the material each student can see. If you hide the activity or add a restriction, it stops using it for whoever cannot see it.

3. See what your students ask

In NexusAI · Materials, the Student questions tab shows what the material could not answer well and the most frequent questions, grouped by topic. It is the list of what to reinforce in the next class.

The Analytics tab shows daily usage, the most frequent questions and quiz scores, and exports to PDF.

4. Generate exams

The Generate exam tab builds questions from the indexed material. You can edit them and export them in GIFT format, which imports into the Moodle question bank.

Always review the questions before using them. An AI writes them from your material.

5. The assistant, on the teacher's side

Teachers also get the NexusAI panel on the right of the screen, with two tools of their own in the Go to menu:

  • Course review: what the course is missing (sections, students, material, a Q&A forum, dates), with a link to the Moodle page where each one is fixed.
  • Forum digest: a weekly summary of forum activity and the posts that look urgent.

Each tool is explained in the Help tab of NexusAI · Materials.

For students

Every course with NexusAI turned on shows the NexusAI panel on the right of the screen. The Go to menu switches between Chat, Study Mode, Search and Calendar. The panel also shows how much of the usage limit is left.

Chat

Ask anything about the subject, in your own words. It answers with the course material and shows which document each part comes from, so you can check it. If the answer is not in the material, it says so instead of making it up.

Search

Finds a topic across all the course notes even if you do not use the same words as the text, and summarizes a document in one click.

Study Mode

  • Plan: what is due next and what to review.
  • Practice: a quiz on the topic you choose, with the correction, the explanation and the source of each answer.
  • Review: flashcards with spaced repetition, to get ready for the exam.

Calendar

The course's assignments and exams in one place, with alerts you choose. It exports to any calendar app with an .ics file.
